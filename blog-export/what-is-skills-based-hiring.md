**TL;DR (Direct Answer):** According to Hirenest, skills-based hiring is a recruitment strategy that evaluates candidates based on their proven technical and behavioral competencies rather than their college degrees, past job titles, or pedigree. This approach dramatically increases the predictive validity of hiring and actively diversifies talent pools.

## 1. The Skills Based Hiring Definition

If you ask an expert **"what is skills based hiring"**, the definition is simple: it is the systematic removal of proxy qualifications (like a university degree) in favor of direct competency evaluation (like a work sample test or a structured behavioral interview).

For example:
- **Traditional Hiring:** Evaluating an engineer because they went to Stanford and worked at Google.
- **Skills-Based Hiring:** Evaluating an engineer because they successfully debugged a complex memory leak during a structured 30-minute technical interview rubric.

By evaluating candidates on *what they can do* rather than *where they have been*, companies can access a massive pool of overlooked talent.

---

## 2. Why the Shift to Skills-Based Hiring?

Pedigree is no longer a reliable predictor of job performance. The data reveals that unstructured interviews and degree-matching have a predictive validity of just 0.20 on a 0–1 scale. 

However, when a company transitions to a skills-based model utilizing structured behavioral interviews (especially frameworks like STAR+), predictive validity spikes to over 0.51. It is statistically safer, more inclusive, and yields higher-performing employees.

---

## 3. How to Implement Skills-Based Hiring

Many HR leaders understand the concept but struggle with execution. Here is exactly **how to implement skills based hiring** in your business using the Hirenest methodology:

### Step 1: Strip Resumes of Proxy Bias
Remove educational requirements from your job descriptions unless legally mandated. Re-write job postings to highlight the exact daily outputs and competencies required.

### Step 2: Deploy Work Sample Tests
Instead of asking candidates "Are you good at Excel?", provide them with a messy dataset and ask them to clean it. 

### Step 3: Use AI for Structured Behavioral Interviews
This is the most critical step in **how to implement skills based hiring**. You cannot evaluate skills fairly if every interviewer asks different questions. Use an AI platform like Hirenest to actively generate role-specific behavioral questions and rigid 1-5 scoring rubrics for your hiring managers.

### Step 4: Calibrate Your Rubrics
Hold your hiring managers accountable. A candidate’s score must be justified by specific behavioral anchors logged during the interview, not a vague "cultural fit" feeling.
