### Key Principles of AI Governance
The White House's playbook outlines several key principles that will guide the development and deployment of AI systems. These include:
* **Transparency**: AI systems should be designed to provide clear and understandable explanations of their decision-making processes.
* **Accountability**: Developers and deployers of AI systems should be held accountable for the consequences of their actions.
* **Fairness**: AI systems should be designed to avoid bias and discrimination.
* **Security**: AI systems should be designed to protect against cyber threats and data breaches.
