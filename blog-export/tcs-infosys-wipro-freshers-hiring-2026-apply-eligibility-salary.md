## The Honest Preparation Strategy That Works for All Three

Here is what separates candidates who clear these companies from those who keep reattempting.

### Preparation Area 1: Aptitude (Non-Negotiable)

All three companies begin with aptitude screening. The topics are consistent:
- **Quantitative Ability**: Percentages, profit and loss, time-speed-distance, ratio-proportion, simple and compound interest
- **Logical Reasoning**: Pattern recognition, blood relations, seating arrangement, syllogisms
- **Verbal Ability**: Reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction

**Time:** Practice with a stopwatch. These tests are as much about speed as accuracy. Use IndiaBIX or PrepInsta for timed practice sessions.

### Preparation Area 2: Coding Fundamentals

You need to be comfortable writing code, not just reading it. Practice:
- Arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks, queues
- Sorting and searching algorithms
- Basic dynamic programming problems
- SQL queries (important for all three)

**Platform:** LeetCode Easy and Medium level problems for 45 days will get you to the required level for TCS, Infosys, and Wipro. HackerRank is the practice platform most aligned with the actual test environments.

### Preparation Area 3: Your Projects — This Is Underrated

In the technical interview, you will be asked about your projects. Most candidates have projects on their resume but cannot explain them clearly. Prepare a 2-minute structured explanation of every project:
1. What problem does it solve?
2. What technology stack did you use and why?
3. What was your specific contribution?
4. What was the result or outcome?
5. What would you improve if you had more time?

The interviewer is not checking whether the project is impressive. They are checking whether you actually built it and understood what you were doing.

### Preparation Area 4: Communication Skills

Wipro and Infosys both heavily weight communication in their selection process. Practice speaking clearly about technical topics with a non-technical friend. If they understand your explanation, your communication is at the right level.

### What NOT to Do

Do not memorize interview questions from "TCS interview questions 2023" lists. Interviewers in 2026 have updated their question banks. Focus on fundamentals and genuine understanding — you will be able to answer any variation of a question if you understand the underlying concept.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which is better for freshers — TCS, Infosys, or Wipro?**
All three are excellent starting points. TCS offers the largest scale and most structured training. Infosys has the strongest brand for AI/digital roles and the best-in-class training at Mysuru. Wipro is most flexible about college tier and offers good growth opportunities. Apply to all three simultaneously — there is no reason to choose just one.

**Q: What is the TCS NQT and how do I register for it?**
TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test) is TCS's primary fresher hiring test. The April 2026 cycle registration is open at nextStep.tcs.com with a last date of April 2, 2026. The test covers Verbal Ability, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, and Programming. Scorecard is valid for 2 years and accepted by multiple companies beyond TCS.

**Q: What percentage is required for TCS, Infosys, and Wipro hiring?**
TCS requires 60% in Class X, XII, and graduation. Infosys requires 60% in Class X and XII and 65% in B.Tech. Wipro requires 60% aggregate in graduation with 60% in Class X and XII. No active backlogs are permitted at any of the three companies.

**Q: Can students from Tier 2 and Tier 3 colleges get placed in TCS, Infosys, or Wipro?**
Yes. All three companies hire through off-campus drives that are open to graduates from all colleges. TCS NQT, Infosys InfyTQ, and Wipro NLTH are specifically designed to identify talent independent of college reputation. Preparation quality matters far more than college tier for these companies.

**Q: What is the salary for freshers at TCS Infosys Wipro in 2026?**
TCS fresher salary starts at ₹3.36 LPA (standard) to ₹6.5–7 LPA (elite roles). Infosys starts at ₹3.6 LPA (Systems Engineer) to ₹11 LPA (Digital Specialist). Wipro starts at ₹3.5 LPA (Project Engineer) to ₹7–10 LPA (Turbo program). All figures are CTC including all components.

**Q: How should I prepare for TCS, Infosys, and Wipro in 30 days?**
Week 1–2: Aptitude preparation — quantitative, logical, verbal (IndiaBIX + PrepInsta, timed practice). Week 2–3: Coding — practice 30 LeetCode Easy + 15 Medium problems, focus on arrays, strings, and basic algorithms. Week 3–4: Technical interview preparation — revise DBMS, OOPs, OS basics, and prepare structured explanations of all your projects. Throughout: Communication practice — explain technical topics to non-technical people.

**Q: Does TCS, Infosys, or Wipro charge fees for the selection process?**
No. TCS, Infosys, and Wipro do not charge any fees at any stage of the recruitment process. These companies are not affiliated with any agent or agency to conduct interviews or make employment offers. **Q: Can 2024 batch graduates still apply to TCS and Infosys in 2026?**
Yes. TCS NQT 2026 is open to 2024, 2025, and 2026 batch graduates. Infosys's current off-campus drive is for 2024 and 2025 batch graduates. Wipro NLTH accepts recent graduates within 2 years of passing. Apply immediately — batch eligibility windows close.

**Q: What happens after clearing the TCS NQT?**
After clearing the NQT written test, selected candidates are called for a Technical Interview followed by an HR Interview. Candidates who clear all rounds receive an offer letter via email. Joining dates vary by batch — typically 3–6 months after the offer.

**Q: Is it possible to get into TCS, Infosys, or Wipro without coding skills?**
Yes, through non-technical roles. TCS BPS hiring (for B.Com, BA, BBA graduates) does not require coding. Infosys hires for business consulting, HR, and operations roles with different assessment criteria. Wipro similarly has non-technical tracks. However, for IT/developer roles, basic coding knowledge is mandatory.
