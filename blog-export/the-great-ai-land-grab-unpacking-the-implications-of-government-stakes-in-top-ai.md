### Architectural Trade-Offs
As AI-powered solutions become increasingly complex, developers must navigate a range of architectural trade-offs to ensure that their systems are scalable, secure, and efficient. Some of the key trade-offs include:
* **Monolithic vs. Microservices Architecture**: Monolithic architectures are simpler to develop and maintain, but may become unwieldy as the system scales. Microservices architectures, on the other hand, offer greater flexibility and scalability, but can be more complex to develop and maintain.
* **Cloud-Native vs. On-Premises Deployment**: Cloud-native deployments offer greater scalability and flexibility, but may pose security and compliance risks. On-premises deployments, on the other hand, offer greater control and security, but may be more expensive and less scalable.
