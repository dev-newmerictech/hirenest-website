## 1. Why Most Testing Methods Fall Short

Here is an uncomfortable truth about recruitment: many pre-employment assessments optimize for ease of administration, not predictive validity. 

If you just give candidates a generic multiple-choice quiz, you might rule out the completely unqualified, but you rarely identify the exceptional. The research shows that:

- General cognitive ability tests: **~0.26 predictive validity**
- Work sample tests: **0.54 predictive validity**
- Structured behavioral interviews: **0.51 predictive validity**

This is the exact divide between **TestGorilla** and **Hirenest**. One focuses on asynchronous, standardized testing. The other focuses on structuring the live interview and work sample evaluation.

## 3. The 2 Types of Evaluation Platforms

### Type 1: Asynchronous Assessment (TestGorilla)
TestGorilla asks candidates to complete predefined assignments without an interviewer present.

**The format:** Candidates take a 30-60 minute test covering specific skills (e.g., Python, Excel) or cognitive abilities before they ever speak to a human.
**Why it works:** It requires zero time from the hiring manager upfront and provides a clear score to filter hundreds of applicants down to ten.
**The limitation:** Candidates cannot explain their reasoning. A brilliant candidate who misunderstands a poorly worded question is disqualified immediately.

### Type 2: Structured Interview Intelligence (Hirenest)
Hirenest provides the framework for hiring managers to ask the right questions during live interviews.

**The format:** "Here are the 5 behavioral questions and the exact 1-5 scoring rubric you should use while interviewing this candidate."
**Why it works:** Structured interviews and guided work samples reveal actual skill and thought process. Hirenest helps managers probe with the STAR+ framework (adding Reflection and Verification) to ensure deep insight.
**The limitation:** It still requires the hiring manager to conduct the interview and actively participate.

## 4. The 1–5 Scoring Rubric: How Hirenest Does It Better

TestGorilla gives you a percentage score (e.g., 85%). Hirenest builds qualitative rubrics so you understand *why* a candidate scored highly. 

Hirenest generates rubrics similar to this for every behavioral question:
*   **5 (Exceptional):** The candidate provides a specific, detailed example with clear actions and measurable results. Demonstrated advanced context.
*   **3 (Adequate):** The candidate provides an example but it is less specific, the actions are less clear. Basic competency.
*   **1 (Poor):** The candidate struggles to provide a specific example, or the example demonstrates a lack of competency.

## 7. Glossary

**Predictive Validity:** A statistical measure of how well a hiring tool predicts actual job performance. Structure is king here.
**Behavioral Interview Question:** A question based on past behavior. Format: "Tell me about a time you..."
**Situational Interview Question:** A theoretical scenario test. Format: "Imagine you're [scenario]. What would you do?"
