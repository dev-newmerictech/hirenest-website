## What Google Just Did to the AI Creative Market

On February 25, 2026, Google announced a major overhaul of Flow — its AI creative studio that originally launched as a video-only tool under Google Labs.

The update is structural, not cosmetic. Google had four separate AI creative tools doing overlapping things: ImageFX for text-to-image generation, Whisk for image remixing and style transfer, Flow for video generation, and Nano Banana as the image model powering parts of the stack. All living in different places, requiring you to export and re-import assets constantly.

That era is over. Everything now lives in a single workspace at flow.google — with Whisk and ImageFX being absorbed starting in March 2026.

The scale of adoption is striking: Flow users have already created over 1.5 billion images and videos. For a Google Labs project, that signals serious investment rather than an experiment.

## What Is Nano Banana 2?

Nano Banana 2 is the image generation engine at the heart of the new Flow. Built on the Gemini 3.1 Flash architecture, it combines high visual quality with the speed needed for fast creative iteration.

**Key capabilities:**
- Resolutions from 512px up to 4K
- Character consistency across up to 5 characters in a single workflow
- Object fidelity for up to 14 objects
- Accurate, legible text rendering in generated images
- Real-world knowledge integration via Gemini's web search
- SynthID watermarks and C2PA Content Credentials on all outputs

**For hiring teams:** The text rendering improvement alone is significant. Generating job posting banners, "We're Hiring" social graphics, onboarding welcome cards, or employee spotlight content no longer requires a graphic designer for every iteration.

**Price:** Free. Unlimited image generation for all users.

## What Is Veo 3.1?

Veo 3.1 is Google's video generation model powering Flow's video capabilities.

**Specs:** 720p or 1080p at 24fps, with upscaling to 4K. Videos of 4, 6, or 8 seconds, extendable to 60+ seconds via clip chaining.

**The standout feature: native audio generation.** Three types of audio are generated simultaneously with the video:
- Dialogue and speech (with lip-sync)
- Sound effects timed to visual events
- Ambient background atmosphere

No competitor does this natively. Runway's Gen-3 does not generate audio. Sora's audio capabilities are limited. Adobe Firefly's video features include no audio generation at all.

**For hiring teams:** A 15-second employer brand video with music, ambient office sounds, and spokesperson dialogue — generated from a text description, no production budget required.

## The New Editing Toolkit

Three features in Flow's updated editor stand out:

**Lasso tool:** Select any region of an image with a freehand selection, then apply conversational prompts to just that area. "Remove the generic stock photo background." "Add the company logo here." "Make the sky sunset colors." This replaces what previously required Photoshop generative fill.

**Clip extension:** Select any generated video and instruct Flow to generate what happens next. The model maintains visual coherence — characters, environments, lighting, and style carry through the extension. Build 60-second sequences from successive 8-second clips.

**Camera controls:** Direct shots with specific movements — pans, tilts, zooms, dollies, tracking shots. The difference between "AI demo" and "useful production tool."

## FAQ

**Is Google Flow really free?**
Image generation with Nano Banana 2 is genuinely free with no daily limits mentioned at launch. Video generation and advanced features require paid tiers. Existing Google Workspace users get 100 credits monthly included.

**What happened to ImageFX and Whisk?**
Both are being absorbed into Flow starting March 2026. Existing users get automatic project transfers.

**Can I use Flow for commercial purposes?**
Yes. Google's terms allow commercial use of generated content. All outputs include SynthID watermarks for AI provenance tracking.

**How does Flow compare to Canva AI tools?**
Canva has strong template-based design tools. Flow has more powerful image and video generation but less layout control. They serve different needs — Flow for AI-generated original content, Canva for structured design templates.

**What is the 1.5 billion creations milestone?**
Flow users have collectively generated over 1.5 billion images and videos since launch — a scale that signals real adoption and justifies Google's investment in making it a full product.
