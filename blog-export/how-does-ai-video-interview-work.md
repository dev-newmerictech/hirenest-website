## 1. What is Asynchronous Video Interviewing?

Before understanding the AI layer, you must understand the foundation. **What is asynchronous video interview** technology? 
An asynchronous video interview (often called a one-way interview) is a digital screening process where candidates record themselves answering pre-set questions on their own time, without a human interviewer present. 

While asynchronous video saves HR teams thousands of hours in phone screening, evaluating those videos manually is exhausting. That is where AI video interview architecture comes in.

---

## 2. How Does AI Video Interview Work?

If you are asking **"how does ai video interview work"**, the process breaks down into three distinct, highly engineered phases:

### Phase 1: Prompt Generation 
Modern platforms like Hirenest do not ask generic questions. The AI generates specific, situational prompts (e.g., "Describe a time you had to pivot a major software release timeline due to an unexpected bug"). 

### Phase 2: Candidate Recording
The candidate logs into a secure portal and records their response. The best platforms allow candidates to review their answers to minimize anxiety and capture their genuine thought process.

### Phase 3: AI Video Interview Scoring
This is the true technological leap. **AI video interview scoring** works by taking the transcribed text of the candidate's answer and evaluating it against a highly structured, predefined 1-5 behavioral rubric.
It looks for:
- **Context:** Did they describe a specific situation using the STAR framework?
- **Action:** Did they clearly articulate their individual contribution?
- **Results:** Did they quantify the outcome of their actions?

## 3. The Myth of AI "Facial Scanning"

A major misconception about **how does ai video interview work** is the idea of facial micro-expression scanning.

In the past, some early AI platforms attempted to score candidates by tracking their eye movements and facial expressions to gauge "nervousness" or "honesty." This practice is highly problematic, heavily biased against neurodivergent candidates, and has been largely outlawed or abandoned in 2026.

Modern, ethical **AI video interview scoring** (like the models used in Hirenest) do not grade human faces. They evaluate *competency, communication, and critical thinking* based entirely on the semantic quality and structure of the candidate’s spoken response.
