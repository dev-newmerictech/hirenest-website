## Nano Banana 2: What HR Teams Can Generate for Free

Nano Banana 2 is the image generation engine at the heart of Flow. Built on the Gemini 3.1 Flash architecture, it delivers high-fidelity images fast enough that the creative process does not break.

**The specs that matter for HR teams:**

- **Resolution:** 512px up to 4K, multiple aspect ratios natively
- **Character consistency:** Maintains consistent appearance for up to five characters across a workflow — critical for employer branding where you need the same "team members" appearing across multiple images
- **Object fidelity:** Up to 14 objects maintained consistently in a single workflow
- **Text rendering:** Generates accurate, readable text in images — enables social media graphics, job posting cards, offer letter headers
- **Real-world knowledge:** Pulls from Gemini's live web data, so references to real locations and contexts are visually accurate
- **Provenance:** SynthID watermarks and C2PA Content Credentials on all outputs

**The price is zero.** Unlimited free image generation with Nano Banana 2. No credit system for images. No daily limits. Sign up at flow.google and start generating.

The character consistency feature is the one worth understanding in depth. If you are building an employer brand campaign that shows "life at the company" across job postings, social posts, and careers page content, you need the people in those images to look consistent. Nano Banana 2 does this. Define the characters once and maintain them across a full content series — without a photographer or model releases.

## Veo 3.1: Video With Native Audio for Employer Branding

Veo 3.1 is the video generation model inside Flow, and native audio capability is what separates it from every competitor on the market.

Most AI video tools produce silent video. You generate the clip, then go to a separate tool for audio. Veo 3.1 generates three types of audio simultaneously with the video:

**Dialogue and speech.** Specify dialogue using quotation marks in your prompt. The model generates speech that syncs with character lip movements. A recruiting video where a "hiring manager" introduces the role — without a hiring manager, a camera, or a recording studio.

**Sound effects.** Describe actions in your prompt and Veo 3.1 generates matching sound effects timed to visual events.

**Ambient audio.** Background atmosphere generated contextually. An office culture video gets office ambience. A remote work video gets home office sounds.

The clip extension feature removes the 8-second duration constraint. Select a generated clip, tell Flow to continue the scene, and the model maintains visual and audio continuity. Google's demos show coherent sequences extending to 30 to 60 seconds — sufficient for a job posting video or onboarding welcome message.

Stop paying for stock photos showing generic office environments with no connection to your actual team. Generate office and team images that match your brand guidelines, location aesthetic, and demographic representation goals. At free pricing, generate hundreds of variations and select the best.

Generate a 30-second video where a "hiring manager" introduces the team and role using Veo 3.1's dialogue generation. Works best for job postings where the actual hiring manager is unavailable for filming, or where you are hiring at volume across many roles simultaneously. A personalized welcome video for new hires — using their name, role, and start date — generated at scale. Character consistency means the "team" welcoming each new hire looks consistent across all onboarding videos.

### Careers Page Visual Content

Life-at-company photography for your careers page, team profiles, benefits graphics, and culture content. Generate images matching your actual office environments and company aesthetic — without a photo shoot budget.

## What HR Teams Should Not Use AI-Generated Content For

**Representation authenticity.** AI-generated images of diverse teams are not the same as photos of your actual diverse teams. Using generated diversity imagery on a careers page that does not reflect your actual workforce is a misrepresentation that will backfire with candidates from underrepresented groups. Generate supplementary brand content; photograph your actual people.

**Candidate-facing personalization implying human review.** A hiring manager introduction video generated by AI is fine as a produced piece. If it is presented as a personal message from a specific real person who has reviewed a specific candidate, that is misrepresentation. Keep AI-generated content clearly in the category of brand communications, not personal outreach.

**Legal documents and structured interview content.** Job descriptions, offer letters, structured interview questions, and evaluation criteria are legal and compliance documents. These require human authorship and review regardless of how capable generation becomes.

## FAQ

**Is Google Flow actually free for HR content?**
Image generation with Nano Banana 2 is free with no announced daily limits. Video generation requires a Pro or Ultra subscription. For most HR use cases — job posting graphics, employer brand images, careers page photography — the free tier is fully functional.

**Does my Google Workspace subscription include Flow?**
Qualifying Google Workspace plans include 100 monthly credits at no extra charge. Check your plan tier at flow.google. If you are on a standard Workspace plan, you get Flow access without a separate subscription.

**Can I use Flow-generated images in paid job advertising?**
Google's terms of service for Flow allow commercial use of generated content. Job posting imagery in LinkedIn Ads, Indeed Sponsored Jobs, or other paid placements falls within commercial use. Review current terms at flow.google before large-scale commercial campaigns.

**What happened to ImageFX and Whisk?**
Both are being absorbed into Flow starting March 2026. Google is handling the transition with automatic project transfers — your saved generations and prompts carry over.

**Is Veo 3.1 audio good enough for professional hiring videos?**
For internal use and casual candidate communications — welcome videos, role introduction clips, culture content — yes. For high-production employer brand campaigns intended for wide distribution, professional audio production is still the better choice.

**Should we use Flow to generate images of diverse teams for our careers page?**
Use Flow for supplementary brand content: office environments, abstract concepts, iconography. For people imagery on your careers page, photograph your actual team. Generated diversity imagery that misrepresents your actual workforce is a candidate trust problem.

**Can Flow generate content in multiple languages for international hiring?**
Yes. Nano Banana 2 pulls from Gemini's multilingual knowledge base. Text prompts in multiple languages are supported and the model can generate images with non-English text — useful for localized hiring campaigns without a global design team.
