### The Role of Network Slicing in 5G Security
Network slicing is a key feature of 5G networks, allowing multiple independent networks to coexist on the same physical infrastructure. While this offers numerous benefits, including improved network efficiency and reduced costs, it also introduces new security challenges. Developers must ensure that each network slice is properly isolated and secured to prevent lateral movement in the event of a breach.
