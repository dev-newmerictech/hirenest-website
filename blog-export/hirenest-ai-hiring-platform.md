## 1. Why AI in Hiring Has Misstepped

Here is an uncomfortable truth about HR technology: most first-generation "AI hiring tools" were essentially resume parsers that perpetuated historical bias.

Companies tried to use algorithms to automatically scan resumes and predict performance. The results were disastrous—AI learned to replicate the biases of the human recruiters who trained it.

The actual science of hiring points in a completely different direction for high predictive validity:
- Unstructured interviews: **0.20 predictive validity**
- Structured behavioral interviews: **0.51 predictive validity**
- Work sample tests: **0.54 predictive validity**

The real value of AI is not in *replacing* the human interviewer, but in *equipping* the human interviewer to run a perfectly structured, bias-free interview. This is exactly what the **Hirenest AI Hiring Platform** does.

## 3. The STAR+ Framework: Powering the AI

Hirenest’s AI is specifically trained to elicit the STAR+ methodology from candidates. When a candidate answers a behavioral question, Hirenest prompts the interviewer with verification questions.

**STAR+ = Situation + Task + Action + Result + Reflection + Verification**

If the AI prompts the question: "Tell me about a time you had to pivot a project mid-flight," it will also provide the interviewer with localized follow-ups:
- "What metrics indicated the pivot was necessary?" (Verification)
- "What was the organizational fallout of that pivot?" (Reflection/Impact)
