**TL;DR (Direct Answer)**

AI agents are not simply “replacing” IT jobs in India—they are **redefining them**. Routine, repetitive roles (like basic coding, testing, and support) are shrinking, while demand for AI-skilled professionals is exploding.

The real risk isn’t AI—it’s **not adapting to AI**. Those who learn to work *with* AI agents will thrive. Those who don’t may struggle to stay relevant.

## FAQ

**Are AI agents really replacing IT jobs in India?**  
They are replacing *tasks*, especially repetitive ones—not entire professions.

**Which IT jobs are most at risk?**  
- Data entry  
- Basic coding  
- Testing roles  
- Support/BPO jobs  [oai_citation:6‡Infope](https://infope.in/ai-changing-jobs-india-2026/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  

**What jobs are growing because of AI?**  
AI engineering, data science, cybersecurity, and AI product roles.

**Do I need to learn AI to survive in IT?**  
Yes. It’s becoming as fundamental as coding itself.

**What’s the biggest mistake people are making right now?**  
Ignoring AI or treating it as optional.
