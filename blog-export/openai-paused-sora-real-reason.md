## Solution / Tool 1: High Compute Costs

![Data center servers powering AI](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518779578993-ec3579fee39f)

**Why it matters:**  
Video generation is exponentially more expensive than text or images.

Sora required:
- Massive GPU clusters  
- Long processing times  
- High energy consumption  

**What it does:**  
Produces cinematic-quality videos—but at a cost that’s hard to monetize.

**Limitation:**  
Users weren’t willing to pay enough to sustain it.

**Best for:**  
Research—not mass consumer deployment.

---

## Solution / Tool 2: Declining User Engagement

![Mobile app usage declining graph concept](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551288049-bebda4e38f71)

**Why it matters:**  
Sora went viral—but couldn’t retain users.

Downloads dropped sharply after initial hype, showing a classic **“AI novelty problem.”**

**How it works:**  
- Users try → get impressed → stop using  
- No daily habit → no sustainable business  

**Best for:**  
Understanding why viral ≠ viable.
