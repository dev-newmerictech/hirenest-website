## FAQ

**If Google and Amazon gave them the money, who actually owns Anthropic now?**
Anthropic remains a privately held, independent Public Benefit Corporation. While Amazon and Google hold massive minority equity stakes, the corporate governance structure and the "Long-Term Benefit Trust" ensure that the founders and safety researchers retain ultimate voting control over when and how models are released.

**Why is it called an "AI Bubble" if the companies are actually making $30 billion in revenue?**
It is considered a bubble because the Capital Expenditure (CapEx) is scaling faster than the revenue. Yes, Anthropic is making $30 billion a year, but they are spending $100 billion to build the data centers required to keep the models running. The market is betting that the cost of compute will eventually drop, or the capabilities will scale to a point where they can charge $100 billion for the output. If neither happens, the math collapses.

**What does this mean for smaller AI startups?**
It is an extinction-level event for foundational model startups. You cannot build a competitive frontier model in your garage anymore. If you do not have $10 billion to buy an initial compute cluster, you are completely boxed out of the foundational layer. Smaller startups must now build "wrapper" applications or niche fine-tunes on top of Claude, Gemini, or GPT.

**Is Claude Code going to replace human software engineers?**
It has permanently eliminated the "Junior Engineer" role. Writing boilerplate code, unit testing, and basic API integrations are now handled entirely by Claude. Human engineers in 2026 are shifting to "Systems Orchestrators," responsible for defining the architecture and auditing the security of the code the AI generates.
