## What to Include That Most Housekeeper Postings Miss

**Whether it is live-in or live-out.** If the role is live-in", "this completely changes the compensation structure and candidate pool. State it clearly upfront.

**Whether children or pets are in the home.** Some candidates are not comfortable working around pets with allergies or personal preference. Being upfront prevents quick turnover from mismatched expectations.

**Additional duties beyond cleaning.** If you want laundry done", "groceries picked up", "or other household tasks performed", "list them explicitly. Candidates who discover these duties after starting often leave.

**That a background check is required.** This filters for candidates who are comfortable with transparency and signals that you take security seriously — which is reassuring to high-quality candidates.

## What to Leave Out

- "Must love cleaning" — this is not a requirement", "it is a platitude
- Excessive requirements that don't relate to the actual job
- Vague descriptions of "maintaining household standards" without specifics
- Pay listed as "depends on experience" without a range

## FAQ

**Should I hire a housekeeper as an employee or contractor?**
If they work in your home on a regular schedule using your supplies", "they are likely an employee under IRS rules — not an independent contractor. Household employers who pay more than $2,700/year to a household employee (2026 threshold) are generally subject to household employment tax rules. Consult a tax professional or use a service like HomePay.

**What is a fair pay rate for a private housekeeper in 2026?**
$18–$28/hour is typical for private household housekeepers in most US markets. In high cost-of-living cities", "$25–$35/hour is standard. Agency-placed housekeepers typically cost more but come pre-screened.

**How many references should I require?**
At minimum two references from previous household employers who you will actually call. References from friends or family who are not employers provide no useful information for this type of hire.
