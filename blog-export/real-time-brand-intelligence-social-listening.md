## FAQ

**What is social listening?**  
Tracking and analyzing online conversations about a brand or topic in real time.

**How is this different from traditional analytics?**  
Traditional analytics looks backward. Social listening works in real time and predicts future trends.

**Why is real-time intelligence important?**  
Because markets move instantly—delayed insights are often useless.

**Is social listening only for big companies?**  
No, tools are becoming accessible to startups and individuals as well.

**What’s the biggest advantage of this shift?**  
Speed. The faster you understand your audience, the faster you win.
