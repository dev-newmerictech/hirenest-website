## Why Most Free Job Postings Fail

**No pay listed.** Postings with salary ranges receive 30–50% more applications than those without.

**Wrong job title.** "Team Member Needed" does not appear in searches. "Part-Time Barista - Downtown Chicago" does.

**Vague description.** "Motivated self-starter with great communication skills" attracts no one specific.

**Wrong platform for the role.** LinkedIn for a janitor and Craigslist for a marketing manager are both mistakes.

## Best Free Job Posting Sites in 2026

**Indeed** — Best for volume. Free basic posts reach the largest candidate pool. Best for hourly, service, and administrative roles.

**LinkedIn** — Best for professional roles. One free active post at a time. Higher quality applicants for management and technical roles.

**Google Jobs** — Best for free organic search visibility. Post on Indeed or your own website with proper schema markup and Google picks it up automatically within 24–48 hours.

**Facebook Jobs** — Best for local hourly roles. Post from your Facebook business page. Very effective for food service, retail, and local service roles.

**Craigslist** — Best for trades and service. Still highly effective for janitors, drivers, laborers, and tradespeople. Free in smaller markets, $10–$25 in major cities.

**Your Own Website** — Free and sends applicants directly to you. Add proper job schema markup for Google Jobs indexing.

## How to Write a Free Posting That Gets Applications

**Use the title candidates search for.** "Store Operations Coordinator" → post as "Retail Store Manager."

**Always include a pay range.** Single highest-impact change you can make. Post a range ($18–$22/hour) if exact pay is sensitive.

**Lead with the most important information.** Most candidates read on mobile and decide in 3 lines. Open with: what the job is, where it is, what it pays.

**Be specific about the schedule.** "Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm" beats "flexible hours" every time.

**List only the 3–5 real requirements.** Long requirement lists deter qualified candidates. If it is not truly required, leave it out.

**Give one clear application action.** "Apply here," "Email resume to [address]," or "Text [number]." One option, not three.

## How to Increase Free Posting Visibility

- Share personally on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram
- Post in relevant local Facebook groups
- Ask employees for referrals (the highest-quality source)
- Refresh or repost every 5–7 days to stay visible in search results

## When to Upgrade to Paid

Consider paying when:
- Free post has been live 7+ days with fewer than 10 qualified applicants
- The role is time-sensitive
- You are in a competitive market with many similar postings

On Indeed, $5–$15/day typically generates a meaningful increase in qualified volume. Set a budget cap.

## FAQ

**Is Indeed completely free?**
The basic post is free. Visibility may decline over time as sponsored posts take priority. Free tier is sufficient for many roles.

**What is the best free platform for hourly workers?**
Indeed and Facebook Jobs. Craigslist for trade and manual labor roles.

**Why am I not getting applications on my free post?**
Most common reasons: no pay listed, wrong job title, description too vague, wrong platform for the role type. Fix content before spending money.

**How long should I leave a free post up?**
7–14 days. If not getting qualified applicants after 7 days, the issue is the posting content — revise and repost.

**Does LinkedIn allow free job posting?**
One free active job post at a time for business pages. Visible to LinkedIn's network and indexed by Google Jobs.
