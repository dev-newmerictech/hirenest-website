The current landscape of AI engineering is dominated by the emergence of multimodal AI, which enables machines to understand and interact with humans in a more natural and intuitive way. For instance, the development of multimodal AI-powered chatbots requires the integration of natural language processing, computer vision, and speech recognition to create a seamless user experience.

The advent of cloud computing has been a game-changer for AI engineering, providing DevOps teams with access to scalable infrastructure, pre-trained models, and a wide range of AI services. However, this also means that DevOps teams must be well-versed in cloud computing concepts, including security, scalability, and cost optimization.
